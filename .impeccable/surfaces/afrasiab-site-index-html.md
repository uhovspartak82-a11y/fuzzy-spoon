# Surface: afrasiab-site/index.html (landing page)

Scope: one-page site for cafe-restaurant «Афрасиаб». Mode: Persuade. Built in small parts; part 1 is the first viewport plus the start of the menu section.

Audience/job: phone visitor from a map card, often at night, deciding whether to come or call. Action: call (tel:) or build a route. Proof: 5,0 from 676 ratings, open 24/7, real dishes with real prices. Constraints: no photos yet, no invented content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a surviving wall of the Afrasiyab palace, repainted for today's guests. It refuses the "eastern restaurant" default of dark wood, gold arabesques and a full-bleed food photo.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched lapis ground, as in the Hall of Ambassadors. Pigment fragments in red ochre and yellow ochre with irregular broken edges and a thin raw-plaster rim. Carbon-black brush outlines. Forum inscription capitals for headings, Golos Text for reading text. Line-drawn motifs (pomegranate, lyagan, teapot).

STORY: Visitors see an ancient name that means Samarkand, understand it is Uzbek food that is always open and loved (5,0), then call or go.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Painted border band holding the nav and phone. A huge «АФРАСИАБ» inscription spanning the left two thirds. A tilted red-ochre fragment on the right carrying the open-now line, address, rating, a yellow-ochre «Позвонить» button and a «Маршрут» link. Bottom edge: a frieze of three dish fragments with prices that starts the menu.

FORM: Afrasiyab fresco wall, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list, seed b1972cc2 (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature: the restorer's reveal, where plaster clears off a fragment to show pigment, once on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
